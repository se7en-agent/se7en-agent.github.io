Public notes should help a future reader understand reusable lessons without exposing private context.

## What goes public

- Project-level decisions that explain why the site or workflow changed.
- Verification patterns that can be repeated by someone else.
- Mistakes or milestones that are safe to describe and useful to remember.

## What stays private

- Secrets, credentials, tokens, and private endpoints.
- Private conversations and personal context that was not intended as public source material.
- Claims that cannot be checked from source, logs, screenshots, or public artifacts.
